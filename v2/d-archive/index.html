FILE 01 / CONTENT PRODUCTION

Ideas made
ready for the feed.

Turning a brand’s everyday stories into social content, with attention to both the idea and the execution.

At Hang Heung, my work spans three consumer brands and content for Xiaohongshu, Instagram, Facebook and Threads. I plan around seasonal moments, new products and retail promotions, then follow the work through production and publishing.

My part
Concepts, scripts, filming, editing, copywriting and design-review coordination.
The rhythm
Content calendars, publishing follow-up and weekly performance review to refine the next round.
The scope
500+ content pieces, almost entirely individually handled. One Xiaohongshu post reached 1M+ views.

FILE 02 / CREATOR COLLABORATIONS

People aligned.
Details followed through.

Connecting the brand’s needs with a creator’s voice, and keeping the collaboration moving at every step.

I handle creator sourcing and screening, quotation conversations and cooperation briefs, then coordinate store shoots, content review and reporting. Clear context and practical follow-up help the internal team and creators work toward the same direction.

My part
Creator sourcing, quotations, briefs, shoot coordination, content review and results reporting.
The approach
Review creator fit and content performance, then use the findings to inform future selections and briefs.
The scope
300+ KOL and food-blogger collaborations, almost entirely individually handled.
